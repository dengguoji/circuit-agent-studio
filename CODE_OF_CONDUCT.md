# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Circuit Agent Studio to be a respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous community. Participants should:

- communicate with empathy and patience;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels;
- respect privacy, attribution, copyright, and licensing obligations;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers rather than opening a public issue containing personal or sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately when appropriate, and focused on protecting the community.
